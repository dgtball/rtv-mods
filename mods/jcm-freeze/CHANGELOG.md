# Changelog

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- Freezing the helicopter now also mutes its rotor sound, returning it on unfreeze (matching the BTR behavior).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- "Freeze BTR & Heli" toggle on JCM's AI tab: freezes the BTR and the event helicopter in place.
- The BTR's engine, drive, and road loops are silenced while frozen and restored on unfreeze.
- Configurable hotkey (default Numpad 4) to toggle Freeze NPC without opening the menu.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- "Freeze NPC" toggle on JCM's AI tab: stops NPCs in place by halting their AI update.
