# Changelog

## [1.0.9] - 2026-11-23

### Fixed
- Beer is no longer accepted by the Cat Bed tray and no longer starts the dance from it.

## [1.0.8] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- The special foods can now feed the cat from the Cat Bed.
- Beer starts the dance when fed from the Cat Bed; the other special foods just feed the cat.

## [1.0.7] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Three of the special foods can be placed into the Cat Bed food tray.

## [1.0.6] - 2026-11-22

### Fixed
- Fixed the pale mask that stayed on the cat's face during the dance; the cat keeps its normal markings while the colored light plays over it.

## [1.0.5] - 2026-11-22

### Fixed
- Cats no longer flash white during the dance.
- The dance now ends with the cats gathering, the extra cats disappearing and the cat settling into the box instead of snapping down.

## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- A colored flash pulses from the middle of the group on every beat.
- The cats sway, drift and pulse in slightly different rhythms after the reveal.

### Fixed
- Removed the duplicate ghost cats that used to appear during the dance.
- Cats no longer drop below the box floor after the reveal.
- Cats are no longer left tinted when the dance ends.
- Feeding again during the final moment no longer breaks the next dance.
