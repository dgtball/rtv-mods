# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-21

### Fixed
- The Makarov now fits the knife slot the same way the other pistols do.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- The knife slot now accepts pistols as well as knives.
- A pistol in the knife slot is a full weapon: it draws with the knife key, fires, aims, reloads, and shows its attachments.
